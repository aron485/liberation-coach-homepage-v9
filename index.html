The method

Turn a broad concern into something you can work on.

Most pressure starts as something too big to act on — a whole relationship, a season, a business. The work is narrowing it to a single situation and three honest questions.

1

What happened?

2

What are you assuming it means?

3

What do you need to find out before deciding?

In coaching, you and Victor work through those questions together. Growth can be a change you choose — asking a question you’ve avoided, checking an assumption, taking an action on purpose — then reviewing what you did and what you learned. Part of it is noticing the emotional debt a situation carries before you decide.
